About us

A new marina on Joviedsa Island

Two hundred and fourteen slips and a fuel dock at Moffat Bay, built alongside the new lodge and opening this season.

Who we are

Moffat Bay Marina sits on the south shore of Moffat Bay, on Joviedsa Island in the San Juan Islands. The marina and the lodge above it were approved by the San Juan Islands First Nations Development Committee six months ago, and both are now within weeks of completion.

Everything on the property is new: the concrete docks, the power pedestals, the harbor building and the ramp. We are taking moorage reservations now so that the docks are assigned before the facilities open.

We do long-term moorage only. Every slip here is reserved by the month, which keeps the docks quiet, the parking manageable, and your neighbors familiar. We do not take nightly or weekend transient reservations.

What is on the dock

Fresh water at every finger, 30 and 50 amp shore power, a pump-out at the head of Dock C, and a fuel dock open from 7:00 am to dusk. Showers, laundry and restrooms are in the harbor building, open to moorage holders with a gate code. Parking is one stall per slip, with overflow on the gravel lot above the ramp.

The chandlery carries the basics — line, zincs, oil, filters — and will order anything it does not stock.

We have a small number of side ties longer than 50 feet that are not part of online reservations. Call the harbormaster on Ch 68 or the office line to ask what is open.

Rates

What a slip costs

Moorage is billed monthly on the length of your boat, not the length of the slip. Electric power is a flat charge added to every slip.

Monthly moorage $10 per foot + $10 for electric power A 34 ft boat costs $350 a month: 34 × $10 = $340, plus $10 power.

Rates are set by the San Juan Islands First Nations Development Committee and are the same on every dock. There is no charge for water, pump-out, or the first parking stall. Fuel, chandlery purchases and a second parking stall are billed separately. Nothing is charged online — you settle the first month at the office when you arrive.

Dock rules

A few things we ask

Reserve the size that fits

Your boat must fit the slip you reserve, bow to stern. A 34 ft boat takes a 40 ft slip; we cannot put it in a 26.

Keep the fingers clear

Dinghies, carts and gear come off the dock at the end of the day. Fire lanes and the ramp stay clear at all times.

Tell us before you go

Leaving for a week or a season, let the office know so we can keep an eye on the empty slip and your dock lines.
