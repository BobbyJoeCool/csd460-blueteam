The bay

A working marina beside the lodge

Moffat Bay is a protected bay on Joviedsa Island, out of the summer chop and a short run from the main channel. The docks are new concrete and steel, the fairway is dredged to 14 feet at zero tide, and there is a harbormaster on the pier every day of the year.

The marina shares the point with the new Moffat Bay Lodge, so the fuel dock, the chandlery and the restaurant are a short walk from your slip. Moorage here is by the month, for the season or longer.
