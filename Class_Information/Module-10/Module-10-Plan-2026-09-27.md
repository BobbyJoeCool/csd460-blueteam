## Screen Capture

Sara records and narrates the whole video in one take (or a few takes of her own), so there are no clips from different people to join. The script is `Screen Capture Script.docx` in this folder. It walks through the site as six user flows and covers every page, popup and confirmation screen, plus what we left out of scope and why, and how we used AI on the project. Its "Before You Record" section covers setting up the database and browser before recording.

### Recording guidance

- Aim for about 20 minutes. There's no length limit in the assignment, so shorter is fine as long as every page is shown.
- Talk through what's on screen, and say why a feature works the way it does, not just what it is.
- Slow down on the confirmation popups and messages. They only show for a moment, so pause or hover so viewers can read them.
- If something goes wrong mid-take, pause, go back to the start of that flow and carry on. Cut the mistake out afterwards.

## Extra Work: Feedback and Cleanup (Robert)

This sets up Module 11's Assignment 11.2, which asks us to incorporate the critiques we receive and "document the changes made in a separate write-up".

1. **Collect every source of feedback in one place:**
    - Beta tester answers (`Class_Information/Module-9/beta-tester-feedback.md`, plus the questionnaires coming in this week).
    - Peer tester comments in the Module 8 and 9 test plans. For example, Sara suggested an account dropdown in the header with separate links to Your Account and My Fleet, and a boat-type dropdown with an "Other" option.
    - Robert's Sep 27 site audit (kept in his own notes): 4 High, 15 Medium and 39 Low findings.
    - Other teams' critiques, which arrive in Module 11. Leave room for them.
2. **Turn it into tasks.** One line per task: what, why, which feedback it came from, and who owns it (mostly Robert). Mark each one as "do in Module 10/11" or "won't do, and why".
3. **Put the tasks on the Kanban board as issues**, in the column or label Sara picks for cleanup work, so her weekly update shows them.
4. **Keep a change log as fixes land**: date, what changed, and which feedback it answers. That log becomes the 11.2 write-up, so it doesn't have to be rebuilt from memory in Week Nine.
