This worked as expected. The note I would have on this, as it was not immediately obvious that you could leave the Reservation number blank to retrieve all reservations, is that it might be better to rethink how this page works entirely. Instead of being a "Look up" it should be more of a "My Reservations" page, where once you navigate to it, all of your reservations: past, present, and future: show up and you can filter by Year/Month/Number/Status/etc.
At the very least, there should be a "temporary text" or helper text in the Reservation Number box that says that "Leave Blank to call up all reservations. Because most people don't keep their reservation number on hand.
