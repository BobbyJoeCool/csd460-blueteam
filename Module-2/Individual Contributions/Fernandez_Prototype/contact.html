Reach the harbor office

The office is on the pier at the head of the ramp, below the lodge. If you are on the water, call the harbormaster on the radio before you enter the fairway.

Harbormaster on VHF Channel 68

Hail Moffat Bay Marina on VHF channel 68 for slip assignments, the fuel dock, and help on the docks. We monitor 68 from 6:00 am to 7:00 pm daily.

In an emergency, call the Coast Guard on channel 16 first, then let us know.

Finding the marina

Joviedsa Island is reached by the Washington State Ferries from Anacortes; the marina is at the end of Harbor Loop Road on the south shore of Moffat Bay. By water, round the point and enter the dredged fairway between the green and red markers.
